Each computer you connect with helios login gets a sync key of its own, listed here by its computer ID. Revoke one, and that computer alone stops syncing. You can also make a key for a computer you set up by hand with helios sync -key.

Upload a stats file

For a computer that can't reach this site. Everywhere else, set up syncing and you never need this.

  1. Make the file

    On that computer, run:

    helios export -o stats.json

  2. Upload it within 7 days

The file holds times and counts, never the text of your prompts. A file that was edited after it was made is refused.

API

Read keys let a script, or another site you sign in to, read your own stats: your name and Slack ID, your projects with their repositories and hours, and your hours per day. A read key can't upload, change, or delete anything, and it reads nothing while your account is banned. Folders on your computer are never shown, only repositories.

Using a key

Send it as a bearer token. Any site may call these from the browser.

CallWhat it returns
GET /api/public/v1/meYour name, Slack ID, when you joined, your engaged seconds in the past year, and your last sync.
GET /api/public/v1/projectsEach project: its name, its repository (such as github.com/you/site, or null), its engaged seconds, and when you first and last worked on it.
GET /api/public/v1/days?since=2026-01-01Your engaged seconds on each day.
GET /api/public/v1/users/{handle}Anyone's public profile, as it shows at /@handle. Needs no key.
