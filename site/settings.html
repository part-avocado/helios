Your data

Bring stats in from a computer that can't sync, or take a copy of your stats.

Upload a stats file

For a computer that can't reach this site. Everywhere else, set up syncing and you never need this.

  1. Make the file

    On that computer, run:

    teardrop export -o stats.json

  2. Upload it within 7 days

The file holds times and counts, never the text of your prompts. A file that was edited after it was made is refused.

Export your data

Download your name, your Slack ID, and the latest stats from each of your computers as one JSON file. It does not include your sync key.

Delete your account

This removes every computer connected to your account and all of your stats from this site, and signs you out. Nothing on your own computers is touched. Reviewers keep your Slack ID and a record of what your computers reported: times and totals, without project names. Signing in again reopens the account, empty.
